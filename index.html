About

Context-driven design with a modern sensibility.

I believe architecture should invite people to slow down, connect, and notice the small moments. I document each project with notes, diagrams, and photography to capture the process behind the finished work.

Design ethos

Human-scale details, simple materials, and daylight-first planning. I enjoy balancing bold gestures with warm textures and quiet corners.

Tools

Rhino, Revit, SketchUp, Adobe Suite, and a sketchbook that follows me everywhere. I prototype with foam models and digital twins.

Collaborations

Open to co-designing student competitions, exhibitions, and research on sustainable urbanism.

Academic 2024

Nordic Library

A library that wraps visitors in timber, soft daylight, and visual connections to the city’s waterfront. The central atrium acts as a social spine with bridges, stacked reading decks, and skylights.

  • Timber structure with cross-laminated floors
  • Daylight strategy informed by heliodon studies
  • Quiet pods for writers with acoustic fins
Competition 2023

Urban Canopy

A modular shade system that brings green corridors to dense streets. The canopy panels collect rainwater, feed planters, and cast shifting patterns on the pavement.

  • Prefabricated aluminum modules for fast assembly
  • Parametric patterning based on pedestrian flows
  • Community workshops for co-designing public space
Research 2022

Sound & Silence

An exploration of acoustic comfort in shared housing. The design uses layered materials, vestibules, and landscape buffers to create calm interiors in vibrant neighborhoods.

  • Measured sound profiles to tune material assemblies
  • Courtyard landscape acts as natural acoustic shield
  • Flexible partitions for adaptable living arrangements

Current project

Sunken Courtyard — a layered retreat

A community hub anchored by a sunken courtyard, terraced seating, and lush planting that filters light down to the lower level.

  • Material palette: rammed earth, copper mesh, and glass brick.
  • Program mix: workshop, café, quiet library, and rooftop garden.
  • Climate response: stack ventilation through the central void.
